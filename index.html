What this version does

The scanner reads follower and following lists through Instagram’s internal API using your existing signed-in browser session. It reads the account ID cookie, stores scan results and preferences locally on Instagram’s origin, and generates avatars in your browser. It contains no account-changing requests.

Read-only does not mean risk-free: Instagram may limit automated reads, and its internal API can change. Incomplete scans are labeled. Logging out afterward does not undo previous activity.
